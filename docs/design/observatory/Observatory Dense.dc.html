Observatory — density test 34 nodes · 53 wires · 68% zoom

Individual lane routing does not survive this many wires — at 34 nodes the left bundle alone would need 20 lanes and the hop count runs past what anyone can follow. So the harness changes shape with density: adjacent-column wires collapse into gutter trunks, long-haul wires leave the field entirely and ride highways above and below it, and only the wires matching your filter — or touching your selection — resolve into real strands. Everything else is a counted band.

Trunk resolves into strands
A trunk is not a new object — it is the same wires drawn as one band with a count. Zoom past 80% in that gutter and it separates into lanes with no re-layout.
Highways
A wire that skips a column leaves the field and rides a highway above or below it. Short hops stay in the gutter. Nothing crosses a node, ever.
What draws in full
01Filtered relations. Pick types in the bar and only those resolve — everything else stays a band.
02The selection's wires. Select a plate and its harness resolves regardless of filter.
03What's in view. Off-screen wires are not drawn or animated at all.
04Nothing, if you ask. Reduced-motion kills every travelling signal; the hues and dashes still carry the meaning.
