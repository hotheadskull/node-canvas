Rules the ground obeys
Nothing under 60 seconds. Anything faster reads as motion and competes with the wires, which are the only thing on screen allowed to say "look here".
Density is the whole thing. About 460 stars, 190 of them packed onto the Milky Way itself with a gaussian falloff across its width. Radii use rnd()*rnd() rather than rnd(), which biases hard toward small — a real sky is overwhelmingly faint specks with a handful of anchors.
The sky is navy, not black. It runs #03060f to #122448 — a real dark-sky photograph is never neutral, and the blue is what makes the dust lanes read as brown rather than as holes. Stars draw from eight colours weighted toward white and blue-white, with a few warm ones.
The band needs structure, not just glow. A bright core at 46% along it, a rose-tan cast at 44%, a second cloud at 64%, a dark rift running its length and two organic dust knots over the core. Five tile sizes (613/719/827/953/1097) are coprime so the pattern takes hours to repeat.
The band lives in canvas space, the stars in screen space. Pan and the Milky Way moves with your nodes; the five star layers move at 20 / 35 / 50 / 65 / 80% of pan for parallax depth. Zoom scales the band only — stars keep their pixel size.
Every layer is pointer-events:none and composited. Only transform animates — the five star layers each translate one whole tile over their cycle. No filter or background-position animation, which would repaint every frame.
Total contrast budget: 12%. No point on an empty canvas may be more than 12% lighter than #03060f in luminance — the Milky Way core is the brightest thing allowed, and it sits at about 9%. If a plate ever struggles to read, the ground is too bright, not the plate too dark.
The ground stays desaturated so the plates can be loud. All the chroma in this design belongs to the eleven data-kind hues. The sky is navy and the band is dust-coloured — nothing on the ground competes with a saturated plate edge, which is what lets that edge read as information rather than decoration.
Reduced motion freezes everything mid-cycle. The composition still reads — the drift is depth, not information.
Replaces the current single-gradient background and Starfield.tsx entirely. The shooting stars there (shoot-1/2/3) are cut — a real sky does not fire one every thirty seconds, and they pull the eye away from the wires.

Text colour floor
At 8px use #9095bd or lighter (6:1). At 8.5–9px use #8e94c2 or lighter (4.5:1). Never use #4a4f76 for text below 10px — it measures 2.57:1 and is the one contrast bug already shipped.
3 · The port one slot per port — it changes shape, never multiplies
Six shapes, one slot
The slot flares rather than stacking. A bar is 12×4px; two to four wires widen its outer edge to 8px, five or more to 12px, so density is legible without counting anything. Hue never changes — fill means wired, outline means open. Drawn here at about 2× for legibility.
Geometry, literal

flare · 5+outer edge 12px, inner 4px, length 12
glow0 0 7px rgba(hue,.85) when wired · none when open
pip5px circle #ffc94d, offset −4,−5 from the slot
inset2px from the plate edge, centred in the gutter
hit area24×24px invisible, centred on the slot
pitch12px between slots on the same edge
Condensed approach
Incoming strands stay 1.2px and 6px apart for their last 56px — no thick cable, just a tight parallel run. The tie is two 1px verticals 5px apart with a count above it. They then fan into the flare mouth over 14px. Nothing is ever drawn thicker than a single wire.
Replaces the stacked-slot drawing from the previous pass. One port is one DOM node with a variant class, which is also simpler to build.
4 · The wire orthogonal with 45° chamfers · never a curve, never a right angle
Anatomy
stub6–20px flat off the port
chamferexactly 10px, 45°
lane14px pitch, in a corridor
Hop
Verticals hop horizontals, never the reverse. A6,6 0 0 1 going down, sweep 0 going up. An unmarked crossing must never read as a connection.
Junction
One give feeding many takes shares a stub and splits at a filled dot, r 3.4 with an r 6.5 ring at .3. No dot means no relationship.
Corridor
Corridors are derived, not authored: inflate every node rect by 24px, decompose the free space into channels, assign lanes in target order. Recompute on drop; ghost to straight lines during drag.

Every wire is three paths. A halo (same d, stroke-width 7, opacity .09), the base stroke (kind hue at .68–.74 with the kind's dash), and the signal (stroke-width 2.6, a light tint, dasharray summing to 1200, animating stroke-dashoffset 1200→0).
Budget: 8 animated wires. Animate only what is in view AND matches the filter or touches the selection. Reduced motion drops the signal path entirely — hue and dash carry the meaning alone.

The map. Everything at once, spatially, wires visible. The only view that shows relationships. Where you think.

Propositions in order as a curve. Opens on any group holding two or more. For a sermon or an argument, where the sequence is the work.
